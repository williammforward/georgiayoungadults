About the conference

The time is now

Each year we gather together to be WITH Jesus—to be equipped, to connect, and to encounter Him in a fresh way. But the gathering is not the destination. It’s the anchor. Just as Jesus did with His disciples, He calls us to first be with Him, and then to be sent out to live with Him in our everyday lives.

“He appointed twelve that they might be with him and that he might send them out…”

Mark 3:14

This conference is a yearly moment where we come together to sit with Jesus again, to be renewed in His presence, and to be reminded that our calling is not just to gather, but to go. We are sent back into our homes, our workplaces, our families, and our communities, not to leave Jesus behind, but to live with Him there. The life we receive in His presence is meant to be carried into the ordinary places of our lives.

We gather to be with Him. And then we are sent to live with Him.
